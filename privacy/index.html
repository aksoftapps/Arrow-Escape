Overview

1 Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to Arrow Escape, a logic puzzle game for Android published by AKSoft Apps ("we", "us"). It describes how the game handles information when you install and play it.

Arrow Escape is designed to work offline. You do not need to create an account, and we do not operate a backend server that collects your gameplay data. Where the game uses Google services, Google processes that information under its own privacy policy.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, microphone input or advertising ID.

3 Gameplay & Gem Information Stored Locally

Your progress through each game mode, your star ratings, Daily Challenge results, achievements and experience points are stored in the app's private storage on your device.

Gems are an in-game currency earned by playing, for example by completing puzzles, Daily Challenges and achievements, and spent on in-game items such as hints. The game keeps your Gem balance, a history of recent Gem transactions, and a record of rewards already claimed so that the same reward is never paid twice. Gems have no monetary value and cannot be exchanged for money.

This information is used only to run the game. It is not sent to us. Android device backup is disabled for the app, so this data is not copied into your Google account's device backup.

Google services and device access

4 Google Play Games Services & Account Linking

Arrow Escape integrates Google Play Games Services. If Play Games is available on your device, Google may sign you in to Play Games for this game. When you are signed in, the game receives your Play Games player ID and display name from Google.

Linking is your choice. Being signed in does not link your game profile. You can link your local profile to your Play Games account from Settings, and the game asks you to confirm first. After linking, the game stores the player ID and display name on your device so that progress is not mixed with another account's.

Google processes Play Games sign-in and account information as the provider of that service, under the Google Privacy Policy. Play Games may also show its own sign-in notifications or profile features, which Google controls.

Today the game does not send your progress or Gems to Google Play Games. See Section 8.

5 Family Safety & Parental Controls

The game starts in Adult Mode. A parent can turn on Kids Mode and protect the Family Safety settings with a parent PIN. Family Safety can include play-time limits and restrictions on certain features.

  • The parent PIN is never stored as entered. Only a salted, one-way hash is kept on the device.
  • Kids Mode settings, play-time allowance and usage records are stored only on the device.
  • Family Safety information is never sent to us, never included in any cloud save, and never shared with third parties.

Third parties and data transmissions

7 Network Communication & Third-Party SDKs

Gameplay does not need a network connection. We do not operate any server for the game. The app includes these third-party components:

  • Google Play Games Services (Google). Communicates with Google for sign-in and player identity. Google's services may also process technical information, such as device and app details, diagnostics and identifiers, to operate, secure and improve the service.
  • Google Play Billing Library (Google). Included in the app, but purchases are not enabled today (Section 10).
  • Android Jetpack and Kotlin libraries (Google, JetBrains). Used to build and run the game on your device.

The game contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDK. Google Play and Android may separately collect diagnostics, such as crash reports or app-install data, under your device and Google account settings. That collection is outside the game's control.

8 Cloud Saves — Current Status

Not active

Cloud saves are not available in the current version. Your progress and Gems exist only on your device.

A future version may add optional cloud saves using Google Play Games Saved Games, so that a signed-in, linked player can restore progress and Gems on another device. If this is introduced, the snapshot would be stored by Google in your Play Games account and would contain game progress and Gem information. Family Safety settings and the parent PIN would never be included. We will update this policy before cloud saves are switched on.

9 Advertising — Current Status

Not active

The current version shows no advertisements and contains no advertising SDK. A future version may offer optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch in exchange for Gems. Before any advertising is enabled, we will update this policy to describe the advertising provider, the data it processes, and the consent choices offered.

10 In-App Purchases — Current Status

Not active

Gem purchases are not available in the current version. If in-app purchases are introduced, they will be processed by Google Play under Google's terms. We would never receive your payment card details. We will update this policy before purchases are enabled.

Data control and legal notices

11 Data Sharing & Third-Party Processing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information, and we do not receive your gameplay data. The only third party involved is Google, as the provider of Google Play Games Services and the Google Play platform (Sections 4 and 7). Google processes information under its own privacy policy.

We may disclose information only if the law requires it. Because we do not hold your gameplay data, there is normally nothing for us to disclose.

12 Data Retention & Deletion

  • Information stored by the game stays on your device until you delete it.
  • To delete it, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the game. This permanently removes your progress, Gems, linked-profile information and Family Safety settings. It cannot be recovered, because no cloud copy exists today.
  • Google Play Games account data is managed by Google. You can review or delete it through your Google account and Play Games settings.

13 Children's Privacy & Parental Considerations

Arrow Escape is a puzzle game. Kids Mode lets a parent set up a more restricted experience on a shared or child's device. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game itself collects no personal information from any player.

Google Play Games sign-in is subject to Google's own age and account rules. If you are a parent and have a question about your child's use of the game, contact us (Section 17).

14 Security

Game data is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read under Android's normal security model. The parent PIN is stored only as a salted, iterated hash. No method of storage is perfectly secure. A rooted or modified device, or someone with physical access to your unlocked device, may be able to read or change local data.

15 International Processing

We do not transfer your gameplay data anywhere, because it stays on your device. If you use Google Play Games Services, Google may process related information in countries other than your own, as described in Google's privacy policy.

16 Changes to This Policy

We will update this policy when the game's data handling changes, and always before cloud saves, advertising or in-app purchases are enabled. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the current version. Significant changes may also be noted in the game's store listing or release notes.
